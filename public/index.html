Ballpark Bingo

How this works

One round per inning. Read it once, then put the phone down and watch the game.

1 Tap a square when you see it happen. The middle is free. Five in a row is bingo, and it ends the inning on the spot.
2 The server decides, not your phone. A tap paints straight away, but if the server disagrees it snaps back. That is normal.
3 You get one accusation per inning. Name whoever you think is furthest along. Right and their card wipes. Wrong and yours does.
4 Accusing wins you nothing directly — it just knocks the leader back so you have room. Bingo is the only way to win.
5 Nobody's score is shown. The card border tells you how close the room is to a bingo, but never who. Work that out by watching people.
6 The feed names who accused, never who they accused. When your own card wipes, only you know it happened.
7 Anyone can start the next inning. Nobody is in charge.
