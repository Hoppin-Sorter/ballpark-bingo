Ballpark Bingo

How this works

One round per inning. Read it once, then put the phone down and watch the game.

1 Tap a square when you see it happen. The middle is free. Five in a row is bingo — it ends the inning on the spot, and it is the only way to win.
2 You get one accusation per inning. Name whoever you think is furthest ahead. Right and their card wipes, wrong and yours does. You win nothing either way — you just knock the leader back.
3 Nobody's score is shown. The card border tells you how close the room is to a bingo, but never who. Work that out by watching people.
4 Anyone can start the next inning. Nobody is in charge.
